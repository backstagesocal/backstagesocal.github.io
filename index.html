Questions

Good to know

Will the replies sound like a robot?

No. Every reply is personal: it uses the reviewer's name and mentions what they actually ordered or said. In the first week you approve the tone so it sounds like you.

Do I lose control of my accounts?

Never. You add Backstage as a manager, so you stay the owner, and you can remove our access with one click at any time.

What if I get a really bad review?

We reply calmly and professionally, and we message you the same day so you know about it. We never argue with customers or offer refunds on your behalf without asking.

Do we need to meet?

No. Backstage is fully online, so everything happens by email or text at times that work around your service.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. It's month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.
